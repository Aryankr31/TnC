02Privacy notice under the DPDP Act, 2023How personal data is used＋

UTIPFL provides this notice before or when requesting consent to process digital personal data through its WhatsApp chatbot and Meta Flows.

Personal data may be processed for NPS transactions, account servicing, identity verification, subscriber support and regulatory reporting. Subject to applicable law, subscribers may request access, correction or erasure, withdraw consent, and raise grievances.

UTIPFL determines the purposes and means of processing for which it acts as Data Fiduciary. Processing is intended to follow applicable legal requirements.

04Purposes of processingWhy the information is used＋

NPS account servicing

Subscriber registration, Tier I and Tier II contributions, scheme-choice changes, address updates and PRAN statement requests.

Regulatory compliance

Meeting applicable statutory and regulatory obligations, including relevant PFRDA, PMLA and Central Recordkeeping Agency requirements.

Communication and authentication

Sending transaction receipts, OTP verification messages, service alerts and statement summaries to the subscriber's WhatsApp account.

05WhatsApp Meta FlowsHow submitted form information is handled＋

Meta Flows are structured forms displayed within WhatsApp. When you complete and submit a flow:

  • Information is transmitted to the service's backend systems for the requested task.
  • Transport security is used to protect data in transit.
  • Meta/WhatsApp provides the messaging and flow infrastructure. UTIPFL states that it does not authorise independent advertising or commercial profiling using subscriber financial data.
06Your data rightsConsent, access, correction and nomination＋

Withdraw consent

You may request withdrawal through the WhatsApp chat (for example, by sending “STOP” or “REVOKE”) or by contacting contact@utipf.co.in. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal. Legal retention requirements may still apply.

Access, correction and erasure

You may request information about your personal data and request correction, updating or erasure, subject to applicable law.

Nomination

Where provided under applicable law, you may nominate a person to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.
