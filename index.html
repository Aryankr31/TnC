Governing the Use of UTI Pension Fund Limited WhatsApp Conversational Banking and Interactive Meta Flows Infrastructure

1. Overview and Scope of Agreement

This legal document constitutes an electronic disclosure executed in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, and guidelines promulgated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). This document governs the access to and use of the automated WhatsApp Business Chatbot and interactive Meta Flows services operated by UTI Pension Fund Limited (hereinafter referred to as "UTIPFL", "Company", "We", or "Us").

UTIPFL is a public limited company incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956/2013, bearing Corporate Identity Number (CIN) U66020MH2007GOI176785, and operating as a wholly owned subsidiary of UTI Asset Management Company Limited. UTIPFL is duly registered with PFRDA as a Point of Presence (POP) bearing Registration Number POP336042022 (granted on April 20, 2022) and manages National Pension System (NPS) assets across Government and Private sectors.

No physical signature or interactive input is required on this web page. Consent is rendered through affirmative actions within the WhatsApp messaging interface.

2. Statutory Notice under Section 5 of DPDP Act, 2023

In compliance with Section 5 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, UTIPFL issues this explicit Privacy Notice to every individual ("Data Principal") before or at the time of requesting consent for processing digital personal data through our WhatsApp Chatbot and Meta Flows applications.

Mandatory Statutory Summary

UTIPFL collects digital personal data strictly for processing National Pension System (NPS) transactions, account maintenance, identity verification, subscriber servicing, and regulatory reporting under PFRDA guidelines. You retain full statutory rights to withdraw consent, access data records, request corrections, and register grievances with our designated Grievance Officers or the Data Protection Board of India.

UTIPFL serves as the "Data Fiduciary" responsible for determining the means and purposes of personal data processing. All data captured via interactive Meta Flows is processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently in accordance with the statutory framework.

4. Lawful Purpose of Data Processing

UTIPFL processes personal data collected through Meta Flows exclusively for specified, explicit, and legitimate financial service purposes, including:

4.1 NPS Account Servicing: Facilitating subscriber registration, Tier-I and Tier-II voluntary contributions, scheme choice modifications, address updates, and PRAN statement generation.

4.2 Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling statutory obligations prescribed under the PFRDA Act, 2013, Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002, and directives issued by Central Recordkeeping Agencies (CRAs) such as Protean, KFintech, and CAMS.

4.3 Communication & Authentication: Delivering transactional receipts, OTP verifications, service alerts, and annual statement summaries directly to the subscriber's authenticated WhatsApp account.

5. Operational Mechanics of WhatsApp Meta Flows

Meta Flows represent native structured form screens embedded within the WhatsApp interface. By completing and submitting a Meta Flow screen, the subscriber acknowledges that:

a) Data inputted into Meta Flow forms is transmitted securely via encrypted API hooks to UTIPFL backend application servers.

b) Data transmission utilizes end-to-end transport layer security (TLS 1.3) to prevent unauthorized interception.

c) Meta (WhatsApp) acts as a data processor/intermediary providing transport infrastructure, and UTIPFL does not authorize Meta to utilize subscriber financial data for independent advertising or commercial profiling.

6. Rights of Data Principals under DPDP Act

Every subscriber interacting with UTIPFL retains enforceable legal rights under Chapter III of the DPDP Act, 2023:

6.1 Right to Withdraw Consent

Subscribers may withdraw processing consent at any time by sending "STOP" or "REVOKE" within the WhatsApp chat interface or emailing contact@utipf.co.in. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the legality of prior processing performed prior to revocation, nor does it impact data retention mandated by statutory financial laws (e.g., PMLA 10-year record retention rule).

6.2 Right to Access and Correction

Subscribers possess the right to obtain a summary of personal data processed by UTIPFL and request correction, updating, or erasure of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.

6.3 Right to Nominate

In the event of death or incapacity of the Data Principal, the subscriber retains the right to nominate an individual who shall exercise data rights in accordance with DPDP rules.
